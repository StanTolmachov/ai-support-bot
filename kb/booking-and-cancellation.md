# Booking and cancellation

## How to book
Book on our website or in this chat by asking for a human. Walk-ins are welcome if bikes are available, but in July e-bikes are often sold out — booking ahead is recommended.

## Booking reference
Every booking has a reference like PP-1042. You find it in the confirmation email. To check a booking in this chat you need the reference and the last name on the booking.

## Changing a booking
You can change the date or bike type for free up to 24 hours before pick-up, depending on availability. Changes are made by our team.

## Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pick-up — the full amount is refunded within 5 working days. Cancellations later than 24 hours before pick-up get a 50 % refund. No-shows are not refunded. Bad weather is not a reason for a free late cancellation, but we are happy to move the booking to another day.
