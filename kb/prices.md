# Prices

## City bikes
City bike (3 gears, basket, lock): 250 NOK for a day, 150 NOK for up to 4 hours, 1 100 NOK for a week.

## E-bikes
E-bike (Trek Allant+ or similar, range about 60 km): 450 NOK for a day, 300 NOK for up to 4 hours, 2 200 NOK for a week. One charger is included for rentals of 2 days or more.

## Fat bikes and winter bikes
Fat bike with studded tyres for snow and ice: 400 NOK for a day, 250 NOK for up to 4 hours. Only available in the winter season.

## Kids and accessories
Kids bike (ages 6–12): 150 NOK for a day.
Child seat or trailer: 100 NOK for a day.
Helmet, lock and lights are always included free of charge.
Winter gloves and balaclava: 50 NOK for a day.

## Discounts
Groups of 6 bikes or more get 15 % off. Students get 10 % off with a valid student card. Discounts can't be combined.

## Payment
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Vipps and Apple Pay. No cash. Online bookings are paid in advance; walk-in rentals are paid at pick-up.
