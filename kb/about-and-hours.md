# About us and opening hours

## Who we are
Polar Pedal is a small bike rental in central Tromsø (fictional demo business). We rent city bikes, e-bikes, fat bikes for snow and kids bikes, and we sell guided bike tours.

## Locations
Main shop: Storgata 99, Tromsø city centre, 3 minutes from the bus station.
Summer pick-up point: Hurtigruten terminal, open 15 May – 15 September only.

## Opening hours
Summer season (1 May – 30 September): every day 08:00–20:00.
Winter season (1 October – 30 April): Monday–Saturday 10:00–17:00, closed on Sundays.
Closed on 17 May, 24–26 December and 1 January.
Late return after closing time is possible through the key drop box at the main shop; the bike lock must be locked to the rack outside.
