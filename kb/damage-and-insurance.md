# Damage, theft and insurance

## Basic cover
Every rental includes basic cover. You pay at most the 2 000 NOK deposit for damage caused by normal use, such as a flat tyre, a broken chain or scratches.

## Full cover
Full cover costs 60 NOK per day and reduces the amount you pay for damage to 0 NOK. Full cover does not apply if the bike was left unlocked.

## Theft
If the bike is stolen while locked to a fixed object, report it to the police and send us the police report number; you then pay only the deposit. If the bike was left unlocked, the full replacement value is charged (city bike 6 000 NOK, e-bike 25 000 NOK).

## Accidents
If you are injured, call 113 first. For any accident or damage, contact our team as soon as possible — damage claims are always handled by a person, not the chatbot.
