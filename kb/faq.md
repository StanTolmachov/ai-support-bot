# Frequently asked questions

## Can I bring my dog?
Small dogs can ride in a bike trailer (100 NOK per day). Bring your own dog blanket.

## Do you rent bikes for multi-day tours to Lofoten or Senja?
Yes, e-bikes and city bikes can be rented for up to 14 days. Taking a bike off Tromsøya must be agreed with us in advance; a person from our team will help you plan it.

## Can I pick up the bike in one place and return it in another?
Yes, in summer between the main shop and the Hurtigruten terminal, for free.

## Is there a lost and found?
Items found on returned bikes are kept at the main shop for 30 days.
