# Rental rules

## What you need to bring
A valid photo ID (passport, national ID card or driving licence) and a payment card. We take a 2 000 NOK deposit as a card reservation, released when the bike is returned without damage.

## Age
Renters must be 18 or older. Riders aged 12–17 can ride with a guardian's signature. Kids bikes are for ages 6–12.

## Riding rules
Lights must be on in the dark season (October–March) — they are included. Bikes may not be taken on mountain trails marked as hiking-only or off the island of Tromsøya without telling us first. E-bikes may not be ridden on the E8 motorway tunnels.

## Late return
Late return costs 100 NOK per started hour, up to the price of one extra day.
