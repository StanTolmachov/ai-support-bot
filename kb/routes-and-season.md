# Routes and seasons

## Popular routes
Around Prestvannet lake: 3 km, easy, good for families.
Telegrafbukta beach along the south coast of Tromsøya: 6 km from the shop, flat.
Round trip of Tromsøya island: about 25 km, mostly flat, 2–3 hours on a city bike, 1.5 hours on an e-bike.
Across the Tromsø bridge to the Arctic Cathedral: 2 km one way; the bridge has a steep climb, an e-bike helps.

## Midnight sun and northern lights
From about 20 May to 22 July the sun never sets, and bikes can be rented for evening rides until 20:00 and returned through the key drop box. In winter, our guided northern lights bike tour runs on Tuesdays and Fridays at 19:00, weather permitting, 990 NOK per person with fat bike and warm clothing included.

## Winter riding
From October to April, only fat bikes with studded tyres are rented for riding on snow and ice. City bikes and e-bikes are rented in winter only when the roads are clear; ask the shop on the day.
